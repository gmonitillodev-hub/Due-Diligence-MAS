# Due Diligence Memo — Circular Materials
Date: October 5, 2023 | Scope: Company profile, product and technology, commercial analysis, team and organization, evidence cutoff date, exclusions

## Executive Summary
Circular Materials is a company focused on recovering strategic metals from industrial waste, promoting a circular economy through its proprietary technology, Supercritical Water Precipitation (SWaP), which claims over 99% efficiency in metal recovery. The company positions itself as a leader in sustainability, aiming to reduce dependence on imported critical raw materials and minimize environmental impact. However, the investment case faces significant risks due to a lack of substantiated claims regarding technology efficiency, undefined market strategy, and insufficient evidence of environmental impact. The preliminary conclusion suggests that while the company presents an appealing opportunity, further investigation is warranted to address critical gaps in evidence before proceeding with any investment decision.

## Company Profile
Circular Materials is a company focused on recovering strategic metals from industrial waste, promoting a circular economy by transforming waste into valuable resources. The company operates primarily in the environmental services sector and follows a B2B business model. It is recognized as a strategic project under the European Critical Raw Materials Act.

## Product and Technology
Circular Materials is known for its proprietary technology, Supercritical Water Precipitation (SWaP), designed to recover metals from industrial waste with an efficiency exceeding 99%. This technology is a significant innovation in the field of waste recovery.

### Findings
- The SWaP technology utilizes supercritical water as a solvent to precipitate metals from waste materials. [10]
- The technology's scalability depends on the ability to adapt to various types of industrial waste, regulatory environments, and market demand for recovered metals. [11]
- There is limited publicly available information regarding API or developer documentation for Circular Materials, which may pose challenges for potential integrations. [12]
- No specific information regarding security and compliance measures has been found. [13]
- There are no public repositories or open-source projects associated with Circular Materials on GitHub. [14]

## Debate Summary
The debate highlighted several strengths and weaknesses of the investment case for Circular Materials. 

### PRO Arguments
1. Market Positioning and Timing: Positioned in a rapidly growing sector with substantial market growth potential.
2. Proprietary Technology: Claims of over 99% recovery efficiency with SWaP technology provide a competitive advantage.
3. Commercial Upside: Focus on strategic metal recovery aligns with increasing demand in key industries.
4. Environmental Impact: Operations contribute positively to sustainability, appealing to environmentally conscious consumers.
5. Leadership and Governance: Addition of experienced Directors suggests a commitment to strengthening governance.
6. Founder-Market Fit: Specialization in industrial waste recovery indicates a deep understanding of market challenges.
7. Visible Traction Signals: Recognition as a leader in the circular economy suggests potential for partnerships.

### CONTRO Arguments
1. Lack of Substantiation: Claims of market leadership lack specific data or competitive analysis.
2. Technology Validation: Absence of public validation for SWaP technology poses significant risk.
3. Undefined Market Strategy: Lack of clarity on targeted industries and clients creates uncertainty.
4. Environmental Claims: Assertions regarding environmental benefits lack quantifiable metrics.
5. Leadership Transparency: Insufficient information on new Directors raises concerns about effectiveness.
6. Founder Background: Absence of detailed profiles for founders creates uncertainty.
7. Missing Traction Evidence: Lack of specific client testimonials or case studies weakens claims of market traction.

### Judge's Conclusion
The central tension of the investment case lies in balancing promising market potential with significant risks associated with unverified technology and insufficient evidence. Further diligence is justified to address critical missing data.

## Preliminary Conclusion and Next Steps
The evidence suggests that while Circular Materials presents an appealing investment opportunity, significant risks and gaps in evidence necessitate further investigation before proceeding with any investment decision. The following steps are recommended:
- Validate the efficiency claims of SWaP technology through independent testing.
- Obtain detailed profiles of founders and new Directors to assess leadership capabilities.
- Conduct a competitive analysis to understand market positioning.
- Gather quantifiable metrics on environmental impact to strengthen sustainability claims.
- Clarify customer acquisition metrics and program success rates to assess growth potential.
