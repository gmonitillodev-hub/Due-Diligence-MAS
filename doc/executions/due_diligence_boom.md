Date: 2026-11-14 | Scope: Product and Technology, Commercial Analysis, Team and Organization, evidence cutoff date: 2026-11-14, exclusions: None

## Executive Summary
Boom specializes in video collaboration hardware solutions, focusing on simplicity and quality. The company has raised a total of $18.50M USD, with significant partnerships with major platforms like Zoom and Microsoft Teams, indicating strong market potential. However, concerns regarding competition, gaps in technical documentation, and financial sustainability have been raised. The evidence suggests that while Boom has attractive growth opportunities, significant risks and missing information warrant further investigation before making a final investment decision.

## Debate Summary
The PRO case highlights the projected growth of the video conferencing market, Boom's strong product offerings, strategic partnerships, global reach, financial backing, and employee growth as key strengths. Conversely, the CONTRO case raises concerns about market competition, technical documentation gaps, partnership effectiveness, financial uncertainty, leadership experience, and missing traction metrics. The Judge concludes that while Boom has attractive investment opportunities, significant risks and gaps in evidence warrant further investigation before making a final investment decision.
