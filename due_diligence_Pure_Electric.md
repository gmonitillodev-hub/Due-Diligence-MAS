# Due Diligence Memo — Pure Electric
Date: 2023-10-04 | Scope: Product and Technology Review, Commercial Analysis, Team and Organization, Evidence Cutoff Date: 2023-10-04, Exclusions: None

## Executive Summary
Pure Electric is a UK-based startup specializing in electric scooters aimed at urban commuters. The company claims to deliver high-quality scooters with precision engineering, akin to Formula 1 standards. However, the lack of detailed technical specifications and independent reviews raises concerns about the verifiability of these claims. The commercial analysis indicates a growing market for electric scooters, driven by urbanization and sustainability trends, but gaps in financial data and competitive positioning need to be addressed. The team assessment is limited due to missing information regarding team members. Overall, while there are promising aspects, further investigation into customer feedback, technical documentation, and financial metrics is recommended to assess the company's technology maturity and product reliability.
