## Executive Summary
Inkeep is a newly established company focused on developing a multi-agent orchestration platform designed to enhance customer interactions through AI agents. Founded in 2023, the company has raised $13 million in seed funding and serves over 200 clients, including notable names such as Anthropic and Midjourney. The platform features a No-Code Visual Builder and a Developer SDK, enabling both technical and non-technical teams to create and manage AI agents effectively. The global market for agentic AI is projected to grow significantly, presenting a substantial opportunity for Inkeep. ## Company Overview
Inkeep, founded in 2023, specializes in AI agent orchestration for customer interactions, primarily targeting enterprises and high-growth teams. The company has successfully raised $13 million in seed funding from reputable investors, including Khosla Ventures and Y Combinator, which provides a solid foundation for scaling operations and enhancing product development. As of March 31, 2024, Inkeep has reported serving over 200 companies, indicating initial market traction and credibility.

## Product and Technology Review
Inkeep's platform is designed to facilitate the creation and management of AI agents through a No-Code Visual Builder and a Developer SDK, which allows collaboration between technical and non-technical teams. Key features include:

- **No-Code Visual Builder**: A user-friendly drag-and-drop interface for creating agents.
- **TypeScript SDK**: A framework for developers to build custom solutions.
- **Multi-Agent Architecture**: Supports teams of agents for various tasks.
- **Real-Time Data Fetching**: Enables agents to access and utilize real-time data.
- **Integration Capabilities**: Compatible with major tools like Zendesk and Salesforce.

The platform's API documentation is comprehensive, detailing endpoints for managing agents, authentication, and integrations, which is verified as of March 31, 2024 [1][2]. Inkeep also maintains several public repositories on GitHub, showcasing its commitment to open-source contributions, although some repositories are still to be confirmed.
