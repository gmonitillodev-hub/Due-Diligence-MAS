# Due Diligence Memo — Miscusi
**Date:** November 19, 2026  
**Scope:** This memo reviews the product and technology, commercial aspects, and team assessment of Miscusi, with evidence cutoff as of today. It excludes any new research or claims not supported by the provided analysis and debate.

## Executive Summary
Miscusi is a restaurant chain based in Milan, Italy, specializing in artisanal pasta and promoting a Mediterranean lifestyle with a strong commitment to sustainability. The company has expanded rapidly, operating 14 locations, including 12 in Italy and 2 in London, and has raised €35.1 million in funding, indicating significant investor confidence. However, the analysis reveals critical gaps, including a lack of detailed technical documentation, missing pricing models, and limited information about the founders' backgrounds, which raises concerns about operational scalability and team capability. The company's B-Corp certification and community engagement initiatives reflect its commitment to social and environmental responsibility, aligning with current consumer trends. Despite these strengths, the restaurant industry faces challenges such as increased competition and financial uncertainty, necessitating further diligence to address these risks and gaps in information. The preliminary conclusion suggests that while Miscusi presents a compelling investment opportunity, further investigation is warranted to clarify financial performance and competitive positioning.

## Company Profile
Miscusi is a restaurant chain that emphasizes high-quality, artisanal pasta dishes while promoting sustainability and a Mediterranean lifestyle. Founded in 2017, the company has expanded from its first restaurant in Milan to 14 locations within five years, demonstrating robust market acceptance and growth potential. The company has received significant funding, totaling €35.1 million, which reflects strong investor confidence in its business model and growth trajectory. Miscusi's commitment to sustainability is evidenced by its B-Corp certification obtained in 2021, indicating a dedication to social and environmental responsibility [1].

## Product and Technology
Miscusi's product offerings center around fresh pasta dishes, with a focus on quality and sustainability. The company promotes transparency in sourcing ingredients and sustainable practices in pasta production, as highlighted in its impact report [2]. However, the analysis indicates a lack of detailed technical documentation, API information, and pricing models on the website, which raises concerns about operational efficiency and scalability. The company's GitHub presence is minimal, with only one repository related to Moodle, indicating limited open-source engagement [1]. This absence of comprehensive product documentation and pricing information could hinder revenue forecasting and competitive positioning.

## Commercial Picture
The commercial landscape for Miscusi is characterized by its strong market position within the food industry, particularly in the restaurant sector. The company targets individuals and families seeking quality Italian dining experiences, pasta lovers, and health-conscious consumers [1]. Despite its strengths, the restaurant industry currently faces challenges such as rising costs and increased competition from specialized chains [2]. Miscusi's direct competitors include other pasta-focused restaurants and casual dining chains, which could impact its market share and profitability. The company's revenue model primarily relies on dine-in and takeout services, supplemented by loyalty programs and community events [1]. However, specific pricing information beyond loyalty program offers is missing, which complicates revenue forecasting [2].

## Team Assessment
The leadership team at Miscusi includes co-founders Alberto Cartasegna (CEO) and Filippo Mottolese, along with Marco Mattia Garoglio as CTO. While the company has expanded significantly and currently employs 184 individuals, there is limited information available about the backgrounds and expertise of the leadership team, raising questions about their ability to execute the company's strategy effectively [1]. The lack of detailed backgrounds for the founders and a complete list of team members further complicates the assessment of the company's operational capabilities.

## Material Risks
The analysis identified several key risks associated with Miscusi:
- **Financial Uncertainty**: The absence of detailed financial performance metrics raises concerns about the company's financial health and sustainability.
- **Market Risks**: Increased competition in the restaurant industry poses a risk to Miscusi's market share and profitability, particularly as the industry faces challenges.
- **Product and Technical Risks**: The lack of detailed product documentation and pricing models could hinder operational efficiency and scalability.
- **Team Risks**: Limited information on the leadership team's expertise raises concerns about their capability to execute the company's strategy.
