# Due Diligence Memo — Inkeep
Date: 2023-10-05 | Scope: Product and Technology, Commercial Analysis, Team and Organization, evidence cutoff date: 2023-10-05, exclusions: None

## Executive Summary
Inkeep is a startup founded in 2023, based in San Francisco, California, specializing in AI agents for customer experience (CX) and operations (Ops). The company has developed a platform that enables teams to build and deploy AI assistants through a no-code visual builder and a TypeScript SDK. Inkeep has secured $13 million in seed funding from notable investors, including Y Combinator and Khosla Ventures, and has established a client base of over 200 customers, including Anthropic, Midjourney, and Postman. However, specific pricing details remain unclear, and while the company shows promising initial traction, uncertainties regarding financial health, market size, and customer acquisition strategies necessitate further diligence.
