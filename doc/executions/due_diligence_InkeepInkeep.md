# Due Diligence Memo — Inkeep
Date: 2023-10-01 | Scope: Product and Technology, Commercial Analysis, Team and Organization, Evidence Cutoff Date: 2023-10-01, Exclusions: None

## Executive Summary
Inkeep is a B2B company focused on providing AI agents for customer assistance and operational workflows. The analysis reveals a strong product offering with verified technologies such as TypeScript and Python, and key features including a no-code visual builder and integration capabilities with platforms like Slack. The commercial analysis indicates a favorable market environment for AI solutions, driven by increasing demand for automation in customer experience. However, there are significant gaps in financial data, customer success metrics, and transparent pricing, which raise concerns about the company's long-term viability. The team, led by experienced founders, has secured $13 million in seed funding from reputable investors. Overall, while the potential for growth exists, critical risks and missing information necessitate further diligence.

## Debate Summary
The debate on Inkeep's investment case revealed strengths and weaknesses. The PRO arguments emphasized favorable market timing and demand signals, visible commercial traction through partnerships, strong product features, and an attractive business model. However, the CONTRO arguments highlighted the lack of specific market size data, detailed information on partnerships, potential implementation challenges, and the absence of transparent pricing. The Judge concluded that while the PRO case presents a compelling narrative, significant gaps in evidence regarding market validation, customer success, and financial sustainability must be addressed before proceeding with investment.

## Material Risks
| Risk | Supporting Evidence | Potential Impact | Required Follow-up |
|------|---------------------|------------------|--------------------|
| Lack of transparent pricing | Verified claims indicate custom pricing without transparency | May deter potential customers | Further investigation into pricing models |
| Competition from established players | Direct and indirect competitors identified | Risk of losing market share | Analysis of competitive landscape |
| Absence of financial data | Missing revenue figures and funding rounds | Raises concerns about long-term viability | Request for detailed financial information |
| Implementation challenges | Concerns raised about user experience | Could undermine customer satisfaction | Assess user feedback and case studies |

## Information Gaps and Limitations
- Detailed pricing model and tiers for the services offered.
- Specific security and compliance claims related to data handling and user privacy.
- Customer case studies or testimonials to validate the effectiveness of the platform.
- Information on API or developer documentation beyond the SDK.
- Any potential limitations or challenges faced by users when implementing the platform.
- Detailed financial data, including revenue figures and funding rounds.
- Specific customer acquisition metrics and growth rates.
- More comprehensive pricing details beyond the general framework provided.
- Information on customer retention rates and long-term contracts.
- Prior exits of the founders and their relevant entrepreneurial experience.
- Current hiring practices or team expansion plans.
- Contingency plans or additional leadership team members to mitigate key person risk.

## Preliminary Conclusion and Next Steps
The evidence supports further evaluation of Inkeep, but critical risks and missing information necessitate additional diligence. It is recommended to obtain detailed market analysis, customer success metrics, and financial projections, as well as assess the broader leadership team's capabilities. Addressing these gaps will be essential for making an informed investment decision regarding Inkeep.
