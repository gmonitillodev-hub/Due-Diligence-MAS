# Due Diligence Memo — Aiko Solar
Date: October 10, 2023 | Scope: Company profile, product and technology, commercial analysis, team and organization, evidence cutoff date: October 2023, exclusions: None

## Executive Summary
Aiko Solar is a solar energy company focused on the research, development, and manufacturing of high-efficiency solar cells and modules. The company claims to have shipped over 200 GW of products and emphasizes innovation and sustainability in its offerings. However, the analysis reveals significant gaps in independent validation of product performance and a lack of transparency regarding the leadership team. While Aiko Solar has reported strong sales growth and market penetration, these claims are not independently verified, raising concerns about the sustainability of its growth. The absence of detailed technical specifications and customer feedback further complicates the investment case. Overall, while Aiko Solar presents a compelling narrative, the risks associated with unverified claims and missing data warrant further investigation before proceeding with an investment.

## Company Profile
Aiko Solar specializes in solar energy solutions, particularly high-efficiency solar cells and ABC (All Back Contact) modules. The company operates globally, providing solutions for residential, commercial, and utility-scale solar applications. Key findings include:

- Aiko Solar specializes in solar energy solutions, including solar cells and ABC modules [1].
- Aiko offers high-efficiency solar modules designed for various applications including residential, commercial, and utility-scale projects [2].
- Aiko's products are designed to deliver high power, efficiency, and safety, even in challenging environmental conditions [3].
- Aiko serves homeowners, commercial businesses, and utility-scale projects [4].
- Aiko claims over 200 GW of cell and module shipments and has a significant R&D investment [5].
- Aiko has partnerships with various organizations and has completed notable projects such as the Mount Everest solar storage station [6].
- Aiko operates globally with a focus on solar energy solutions [7].

## Commercial Analysis

- Aiko Solar reported record sales in various overseas markets in 2023, particularly leading in sales across South Korea and India [13].
- As of March 2024, Aiko Solar's ABC module products have penetrated markets across 18 countries and regions, including China, Europe, Australia, Japan, and South Korea [14].
- Aiko Solar had a revenue of 5.29 billion CNY for the quarter ending September 30, 2026, reflecting a growth of 67.89% [15].

## Team and Organization
The review of Aiko Solar's team reveals significant gaps in information regarding the founders and leadership structure. Key findings include:

- The specific founders of Aiko Solar are not explicitly mentioned on the official website or in the LinkedIn profile [18].
- There is no information available regarding any prior exits by the founders or key team members [19].
- Aiko Solar positions itself as a global leader in new energy technology, specifically in the photovoltaic industry, with over 16 years of experience [20].
- The leadership team is not detailed on the website or LinkedIn, raising concerns about visibility and robustness [21].
- The official website includes a careers section, but there are no specific job postings or hiring signals visible at this time [22].
- There is a significant key-person risk due to the lack of a visible leadership team and the small number of employees [23].

## Debate Summary
The debate on Aiko Solar's investment case highlighted several strengths and weaknesses. 

### PRO Arguments
1. Strong product offering and market positioning with a focus on high-efficiency solar cells and ABC modules.
2. Significant market demand and growth potential indicated by reported shipments and sales.
3. Global reach and diverse customer base enhancing resilience.
4. Recognition as a Tier 1 manufacturer and holding numerous patents.
5. Partnerships and notable projects enhancing reputation.
6. Innovative solutions aligning with market trends.
7. Claimed experience in the photovoltaic industry suggesting capable leadership.

### CONTRO Arguments
1. Lack of independent validation for product claims raises concerns.
2. Unverified sales and market penetration claims.
3. Key-person risk due to lack of visibility into the leadership team.
4. Insufficient technical evidence regarding product capabilities.
5. Limited customer feedback and case studies.

### Judge's Conclusion
The central tension lies in the contrast between strong market positioning and significant risks associated with unverified claims. Further diligence is recommended to address critical gaps.

- Independent performance validation for solar modules.
- Detailed technical specifications and performance metrics.
- Comprehensive market share analysis.
- Background information on the leadership team.

## Preliminary Conclusion and Next Steps
The evidence suggests that further evaluation is warranted due to significant risks associated with unverified claims and missing data. It is essential to obtain independent validation of product performance, detailed technical specifications, and insights into the leadership team's background. Additionally, verifying sales figures and market penetration claims will be crucial in understanding Aiko Solar's actual market position and growth potential.
