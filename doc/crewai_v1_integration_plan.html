Summary

Replace the Version 2 design (Profiler Crew + sequential Analyst Crew) with one hierarchical AnalystCrew.

The crew holds the manager agent, Company Profiler, Tech, Team and Commercial. Debate and Memo Writer crews stay unchanged.

OP-01 and OP-02 are known issues. Tests must confirm them with traces. Do not hide them.
